FitLog — Workout Tracker

01 / OVERVIEW

About the project.

FitLog is a workout-focused web application designed to make discovering and organizing workouts simple and intuitive.

Users can explore a workout library, open individual workout details and view useful information including muscle groups, equipment, difficulty, duration, calories, sets, reps and instructions.

The application also allows users to build their own workout plan by adding workouts to Today's Plan or saving them for later.
